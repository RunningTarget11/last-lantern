How to Play

Miners drop from the hatch and walk on their own. They turn at walls, fall off ledges, and a long fall kills them. Get enough of them into the lift.

Tap a skill, then tap a miner. The miner under your finger or cursor is ringed in brass.
Climber scales walls. Floater opens a canvas sack and survives any fall. Blaster sets a powder charge (5 s). Blocker turns the crowd (tap him again with Blocker to release him). Builder lays a timber stair. Basher tunnels sideways, Miner diagonally down, Digger straight down. Granite can't be broken.
Lamp gives a miner a Davy lamp. Torch: tap the ground to plant one. Unlit miners in a shadow seam are taken.

Drag to pan, pinch or wheel to zoom, or tap the map. Keys: 1–0 skills, −/+ release rate, P pause, F fast-forward, N nuke (press twice), arrows pan.
